THE PROBLEM

Agents either remember too much or forget what becomes useful later.

Keeping every rejected idea in model context wastes tokens and attention. Deleting it loses the reasoning needed when a price, API, regulation, or capability changes. SPORE stores the detailed evidence outside working context and leaves behind a compact, typed wake condition.
